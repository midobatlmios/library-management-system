## Scope

Home page of the library website (route `/`), mode: Persuade. The same visual world carries the whole site (navbar, catalog, book page, member and admin areas).

Audience: members and visitors of a real local library (Morocco, prices in MAD). Job: find a book and see whether a copy is on the shelf. Secondary: understand how borrowing works and become a member.

Constraints: no cover images, no library name, logo, hours or address exist yet; none may be invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Finding a book is wayfinding. The site is signed like a city in cobalt-and-white enamel plaques; refuses the category default of a search hero over a grid of cover photos.

OWN-WORLD: Cobalt enamel field, white enamel rim inset from a rounded edge, domed steel rivets, enamel white plates; genre plates in green, red, ochre, black and teal enamel. One signage sans (Overpass) in caps for plates, sentence case for reading. Tabular numerals for copy counts.

STORY: The visitor sees the search set into a street plaque, reads genres as street names with live counts, scans shelves of book plates that state copies on the shelf, learns the four borrowing signs, then is invited to join.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width cobalt plaque with rivets: heading "Find your next book", a white enamel search field and Search button set into it, a line stating titles on the shelf now, and the top-level genres as street-name plates with counts. The first shelf row starts below.

FORM: candidate 5 of 7 (Enamel street plaques). seed:c219437c4e8a005f

SIGNATURE: Book plates: every book is an enamel plate in its genre's colour carrying its genre path and live "on the shelf" count; the chosen genre plate is struck forward while all stay present.

## Raises kept

- Every book carries a plate of origin (genre path) and copies on the shelf now.
- Borrowing signed as four numbered direction signs: find, borrow or reserve, pick up, return.
- Each figure owns one truth.
- All genres present at once; the chosen one struck forward.
- Shelves scroll sideways with snap.
